# Weather field recordings

Rain on glass and open-air drips combine recordings by hoshisato, SignatureSoundsOrg and Joseph Sardin. Wind and sand use winter conifers recorded by lwdickens and moving trees recorded by Anya_Media. The fire is ecfike’s campfire, and the snow grains were cut from Sardin’s snow footsteps. [Rain and drips](https://freesound.org/people/hoshisato/sounds/853565/) · [Rain hitting a window](https://freesound.org/people/SignatureSoundsOrg/sounds/869851/) · [Windshield rain](https://bigsoundbank.com/rain-on-car-windshield-2-s1297.html) · [Winter wind](https://freesound.org/people/lwdickens/sounds/259968/) · [Tree gusts](https://freesound.org/people/Anya_Media/sounds/523389/) · [Campfire](https://freesound.org/people/ecfike/sounds/128704/) · [Snow](https://bigsoundbank.com/steps-in-the-snow-3-s3363.html)

The submerged bed uses Félix Blume’s hydrophone recording in Ilo harbor and the current captured by Salomé Lubczanski. Lightning uses three independent recordings by Joseph Sardin and Axeline T. [Sea, crustaceans and bubbles](https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/705054/) · [Underwater flow](https://freesound.org/people/Salom%C3%A9_Lubczanski/sounds/733812/) · [Thunder #2](https://bigsoundbank.com/thunder-2-s3113.html) · [Thunder #3](https://bigsoundbank.com/thunder-3-s3114.html) · [Thunder #6](https://bigsoundbank.com/thunder-6-s3179.html)

Sardin’s gravel, refrigeration and MIG welder recordings provide particles, hull resonance and arcing. kalhan’s cicada wingbeats provide the organic motion in spore bursts. 13GPanska_Langerova_Lucie’s walnut fracture provides the dry close clicks in radioactive wind.
